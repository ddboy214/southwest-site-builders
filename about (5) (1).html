About Valley Roofing & Repair

Dependable roofing service for Phoenix-area homes — honest work at fair prices.

Why We Started

Valley Roofing & Repair was founded because Phoenix homeowners deserve a roofer who shows up on time, charges fair prices, and stands behind their work. Arizona's extreme heat and monsoon storms are tough on roofs — we help homeowners protect their biggest investment.

Every roof we install or repair is backed by our workmanship warranty. We use quality materials, follow manufacturer specifications, and never cut corners.
