About The AC Team

Arizona-owned HVAC company serving Glendale and the Phoenix metro with honest work and fair prices.

Our Story

The AC Team was founded with a simple belief: Glendale homeowners deserve HVAC service that's honest, fast, and affordable. Too many Arizona families have been overcharged or left waiting during brutal summers. We started this company to change that.

Every technician on our team is EPA-certified, background-checked, and trained to treat your home like their own. We don't upsell unnecessary services, and we never charge overtime rates for evenings or weekends.
