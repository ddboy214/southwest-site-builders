About Ufirst Heating & Cooling

Arizona-owned HVAC company putting customers first with honest work and fair prices.

Why We Exist

Ufirst Heating & Cooling was founded because too many Glendale homeowners have been overcharged or misled by HVAC companies. We started this business to do things differently — honest diagnostics, transparent pricing, and workmanship we stand behind.

Every technician is EPA-certified and background-checked. We never charge overtime for evenings or weekends, and we never recommend a replacement when a repair will do.
