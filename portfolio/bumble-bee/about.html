About Bumble Bee Home Services

Locally owned plumbing, HVAC, and electrical company serving Glendale and the West Valley.

Our Story

Bumble Bee Home Services was started with one goal: give Glendale homeowners a single, trusted company for all their home service needs. Instead of calling three different contractors for a plumbing leak, AC failure, and electrical issue, you call us.

Owner Mike Krpata leads every job with the same standard: show up on time, communicate clearly, fix it right the first time, and charge a fair price. We're not a national franchise — we're your neighbors.
