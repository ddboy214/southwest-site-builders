How I Build

Human Refinement

01

What is Vibe Coding?

Vibe coding means I describe what I want in plain English — "a mobile-friendly plumbing website with a contact form, service area map, and emergency phone button" — and AI tools write the code for me. It's fast, affordable, and gets us 80% of the way there in minutes instead of weeks.

02

Then I Refine It Myself

AI drafts are a starting point, not a finish line. I personally open every file, clean up the code, fix mobile layout issues, optimize images, write your actual business copy, set up SEO meta tags, and test the contact form until it works perfectly.

03

You Review & Approve

You see a live preview before anything launches. You request changes. I make them. You don't pay until you're 100% happy with how it looks and functions.

Why this matters to you: You get a professional, custom-coded website without agency prices ($2,000–$5,000+). I pass the AI speed savings directly to you — starting at $500.
