Privacy Notice

Starter notice: This plain-language notice explains the quote-request form. It is not legal advice; review it for your business and applicable privacy laws before collecting customer information.

What the form collects

If you request a quote, the form collects your name, business name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and any message you provide. It also records the time of the request.

Why it is used

Southwest Site Builders uses this information to review and respond to your website inquiry. The information is not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored and who can access it

Requests are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database and can be viewed through the owner dashboard. Access to that dashboard requires a private administrator token. Do not submit passwords, financial account numbers, health information, or other sensitive details in the message field.

Retention and deletion

Inquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage the business relationship. The site owner can remove an inquiry from the dashboard. To ask about your information or request deletion, email devanteallen0@gmail.com.
